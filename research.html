Research

research interests

My current research focus is on planet formation and protoplanetary disks. I previously worked on X-ray binary dynamics, exoplanet searches, asteroseismology in M dwarfs, and the search for extraterrestrial intelligence. For all these projects, I developed tools to analyze large datasets from a wide range of instruments and to extract as much information as possible from them.

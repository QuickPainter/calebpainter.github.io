Caleb Painter

Incoming PhD student in Astronomy & Astrophysics, Princeton University

I am an Astrophysics PhD student at Princeton and an NSF Graduate Research Fellow. I studied Astronomy, Physics, and the History of Science at Harvard, where I graduated summa cum laude. <\br> My research focuses on planetary systems and protoplanetary disks, and I work with telescope data from ground-based interferometers to X-ray observatories. I am specifically interested in the timescales of planetary formation, the mass reservoirs of disks, and the dynamics of older stellar and planetary systems. <\br> I am also interested in the history of astronomy and how it has developed across different cultures. I spent my post-graduate year crewing traditional Polynesian sailboats and studying celestial navigation in New Zealand and Taiwan as a Michael C Rockefeller Fellow.

Other Interests

A few things outside the research, for anyone curious.

Sailing & Traditional Navigation

As a Michael C. Rockefeller Fellow, I spent a year crewing traditional sailing vessels in New Zealand and Taiwan, working alongside Polynesian voyaging communities and training in celestial navigation — wayfinding by star, swell, and sky, without instruments. It's the strand of my life most directly in conversation with the astrophysics: the same sky, read two different ways.

Languages & Travel

I grew up overseas and have lived across a number of countries. I'm fluent in French and proficient in Spanish, and I keep close personal and family ties to France and Taiwan.
